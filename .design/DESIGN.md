## Brand & Style

This design system draws fundamentally from the traditions of classical editorial publishing, archival monographs, and high-end literary broadsheets, infused with the systematic rigor of technical documentation. The brand persona is scholarly, authoritative, intellectual, and exquisitely restrained. 

Rather than chasing ephemeral digital gradients or floating glass surfaces, this system honors tactile physicality: physical paper, dense printer's ink, hairline typographic rules, and deliberate marginalia. It speaks to discerning audiences who value clarity, intellectual depth, craftsmanship, and unhurried confidence.

The design movement is **Editorial Minimalism with Archival Modernism**. Visual hierarchy is achieved purely through precise asymmetric grid alignments, disciplined proportional typographic scale, high typographic contrast, and calculated punctuation of warm terracotta against unbleached warm pulp paper.

## Colors

The color palette is deliberately physical and grounded in traditional print materials. 

- **Primary (`#0d0d0d` / `ink`):** Represents deep, non-reflective printer's ink. Used for high-contrast mastheads, foundational text, authoritative headings, heavy masthead rules, and dense structural elements.
- **Secondary (`#c84b2f` / `terracotta`):** A vibrant yet earthy mineral crimson tone. It functions strictly as an intentional editorial punctuation mark—used for semantic accents, italicized emphasis, structural accent borders, directional arrow glyphs, and active interactive cues. It must be used sparingly to retain its punch.
- **Tertiary (`#8a8074` / `muted-stone`):** A warm, desaturated neutral used for secondary taxonomic indexing, section metadata, labels, category tags, and structural subtext.
- **Neutral (`#f5f0e8` / `paper`):** The foundational substrate. It is an unbleached, warm cotton-rag paper tone that softens high-contrast typographic rendering and reduces eye fatigue while providing natural depth.
- **Rule Divider (`#d4cfc6`):** A subtle, delicate hairline tone that creates quiet spatial divisions without severing visual narrative flow.
- **Deep Neutral Body (`#3a3530`):** Softened carbon ink used specifically for long-form body copy, column descriptions, and secondary narrative blocks to provide optimal reading ergonomics against the paper canvas.

This design system operates exclusively in light mode; dark mode is strictly omitted to preserve the physical integrity of ink-on-paper editorial design.

## Typography

The typographic strategy leverages an intentional tripartite tension:
1. **The Literary Voice (Playfair Display):** High-contrast transitional serif delivering gravitas, expressive cadence, and editorial nobility in display titles and manifesto lead-ins.
2. **The Rational Voice (Instrument Sans):** Crisp, humanist-leaning grotesk providing neutral, highly legible reading comfort for descriptions, analytical narratives, and structural subtitles.
3. **The Archival / Taxonomic Voice (Space Mono):** Fixed-width, technical monospaced notation evoking index cards, archival call numbers, catalog tags, and journalistic meta-headers.

### Typographic Rules:
- All labels and technical badges set in the monospace font must utilize strict uppercase transformations with generous tracking (`1.5px` to `2.5px`).
- Display headlines use tight leading (`1.05`) to create compact, commanding typographic blocks reminiscent of traditional newspaper mastheads.
- Italicization within `Playfair Display` headlines is reserved solely for focal accent words and must be colorized with the secondary terracotta token.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a single-container editorial format constrained to a maximum width of `720px`. The composition utilizes an asymmetric marginalia grid that deliberately pairs catalog indexing with substantive body content.

### Layout Mechanics:
- **Desktop Structure:** Content sections utilize a two-column asymmetric split with an index column of `120px` and an expansive content column (`1fr`), divided by a `24px` horizontal column gap. The left column houses monospaced section tags and taxonomic indicators, while the right column hosts fluid editorial content.
- **Nested Grids:** Secondary content units (such as functional pillars or project matrices) divide the primary column into balanced two-column configurations with a `20px` gutter.
- **Rhythm & Insets:** Vertical section spacing is metered at `36px`. Hairline horizontal dividers punctuate section endings with `28px` vertical breathing room (`28px 0`).
- **Responsive Adaptations (< 520px):**
  - The asymmetric `120px 1fr` split collapses entirely into a single stacked column (`1fr`) with the vertical gap reducing to `10px`.
  - Section labels remove vertical top offsets and sit directly above the content blocks.
  - Header layouts stack vertically, shifting right-aligned metadata into left-aligned chronological order.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects simulated 3D spatial models, Z-plane physics, drop shadows, blurred lighting, and neomorphic extrusions. The entire elevation philosophy is rooted in **flat print surfaces and deliberate structural boundaries**.

Hierarchy and depth are communicated exclusively through:
1. **Rule Thicknesses:** From a crisp `3px solid var(--ink)` masthead rule establishing base authority, down to `1px solid var(--rule-divider)` hairlines delimiting logical structural boundaries.
2. **Left-Border Accents:** Editorial focus cards (such as pillars or selected exhibits) employ a tactile `2px solid var(--terracotta)` vertical border on their left perimeter, offset with a `14px` internal padding, creating an immediate typographic indent.
3. **Contrast Tiers:** Highlighting is achieved through surface inversions (e.g., solid black ink pills with reversed paper-colored text) rather than shadows or elevated planes.

## Shapes

The design language embodies **architectural sharpness**. 

Containers, cards, horizontal dividers, text inputs, and focus areas adhere to a strict `0px` radius. This maintains the crisp lines of traditional bookbinding, metal typesetting plates, and printed broadsheets.

The solitary controlled exception is the micro-radius of `2px` applied exclusively to inline status pills and small technical tags, which prevents monospaced characters from colliding harshly with dark inverted borders while retaining structural tension.

## Components

### 1. Header Masthead
- **Structure:** Anchored by a prominent `3px solid var(--ink)` top rule. Spanning a two-column responsive grid (`1fr auto`).
- **Typography:** Display title on the left; right-aligned monospaced status and title tags on the right.
- **Spacing:** `28px` top padding directly underneath the primary ink rule, with a `36px` margin below.

### 2. Status Badges & Pills
- **Appearance:** Solid ink fill (`#0d0d0d`) with reversed paper typography (`#f5f0e8`).
- **Typography:** `Space Mono`, `10px`, uppercase, `1.5px` tracking.
- **Geometry:** Micro-radius of `2px`, internal padding of `5px 12px`. Inline display with zero drop shadow.

### 3. Editorial Pillar Cards
- **Structure:** Uncontained flat card utilizing an intentional left border: `2px solid var(--terracotta)`.
- **Spacing:** `14px` left padding, zero top/bottom/right borders.
- **Title:** `Instrument Sans`, `12px`, uppercase, `500` weight, `1.5px` tracking, colored in terracotta (`#c84b2f`). Margin bottom: `6px`.
- **Body:** `Instrument Sans`, `13px`, line-height `1.6`, colored in `#3a3530`.

### 4. Selected Work & Archival Lists
- **Structure:** Vertical stack with `10px` row spacing.
- **Item Styling:** Flex layout aligned with start baseline.
- **Glyph Bullet:** Custom monospaced right-arrow prefix (`→`) colored in terracotta (`#c84b2f`), set in `Space Mono` `12px`, separated by a `10px` horizontal gap.
- **Text:** `Instrument Sans`, `13.5px`, line-height `1.55`, regular weight, color `#3a3530`.

### 5. Dividers & Section Rules
- **Standard Rule:** Native horizontal rule styled with `border: none`, `border-top: 1px solid var(--rule-divider)`, `margin: 28px 0`.
- **Footer Rule:** Structural `1px solid var(--rule-divider)` border-top, with `20px` top padding.

### 6. Interactive Links & Inline Emphasis
- **Lead Text:** Large editorial serif with fluid scaling (`clamp(15px, 2.5vw, 18px)`), line-height `1.65`.
- **Inline Emphasis (`em`):** `Playfair Display` italic styled in terracotta (`#c84b2f`).
- **Interactive Links:** Clean inline links with a hairline bottom border (`1px solid var(--terracotta)`), transitioning opacity rather than introducing standard blue browser underlines.

### 7. Form Controls (Inputs, Checkboxes)
- **Input Fields:** Unfilled paper background (`#f5f0e8`), bottom border only (`1px solid var(--rule-divider)`), focusing to `1px solid var(--ink)`. Typography set in `Space Mono` `11px`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Square, sharp-edged (`0px` radius) ink outlines (`1px solid var(--ink)`). Filled completely with ink when selected, showing paper-colored checkmarks.
